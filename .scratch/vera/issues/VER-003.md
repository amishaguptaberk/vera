Title: VER-003 — Model-backed investigation on LangGraph

User value:
When Sam starts the investigation, three specialists actually reason over the charge, the purchase records and related transactions and post grounded findings, and the run survives a refresh or restart.

Scope:
- SpecialistRunner, ModelClient seam (Live and Recorded), ToolGateway with per-specialist scope, schema and grounding validators, bounded retries and explicit failure (plan §18).
- NVIDIA provider per decision D-G, including the capability probe that pins the model id.
- LangGraph graph built from workflow v3 node kinds; Postgres checkpointer; worker job loop and `specialist_runner`.
- Specialists: Charge Investigator, Related-Transaction Investigator (in parallel), then Receipt and Document Reviewer.
- `start_investigation` and `retry_specialist_step` commands; workflow_runs, workflow_steps with execution_mode, model id, prompt version, usage; `GET /api/cases/{id}/runs`; stream events `run.step_*`.
- Recordings for the three specialists and the `record` command.

Non-goals:
- Follow-up drafting, callback, packet and prevention specialists (later tickets reuse the runner).
- Workflow builder APIs (VER-014).

Acceptance criteria:
- start_investigation pins workflow and policy versions on the case and is idempotent on `inv:{case_id}`.
- In REPLAY the run produces the amount, quantity and missing-receipt findings, each citing record id, version and field, and the related-charge finding that links CHG-1051 by full transaction identity and never by last four digits (US-C03).
- A finding whose cited value does not match the source field is rejected and triggers a validation retry; after two failures the step is `failed` with reason `ungrounded` and the case stays in its step with a retry action.
- A tool call outside the specialist's declared records raises scope_violation and is recorded.
- Killing the worker mid-run and restarting resumes from the checkpoint without duplicating findings or events (fixture 38).
- A LIVE failure is never replaced by a recording unless retry_specialist_step is called with mode replay; that step is then stored and labeled as replayed.
- A missing recording in REPLAY is an error.
- No model output changes state, step or policy except through typed records and derive_step.

Dependencies:
- VER-002
- VER-004

Test-first behavior:
A replay test that starts the investigation and asserts the persisted findings, their citations, the step sequence and event order; then a test with a corrupted recording asserting the ungrounded failure path.

Demo proof:
Start the investigation in LIVE on a machine with the key and watch three specialists post findings; repeat in REPLAY and show identical contracts.
