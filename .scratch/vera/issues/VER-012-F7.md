Title: VER-012-F7 — Decision tab and reviewer screen

User value:
Dana reviews one packet, asks Vera about it, and approves or returns with explicit controls.

Scope:
- Decision panel, credit timeline, packet view, reviewer route with approve and return (reason, next step, owner), stale banner, approval history.
- Follow docs/frontend-architecture.md for layer rules, state ownership and parity method; the prototype is the visual reference and is not redesigned.

Non-goals:
- Card-admin controls.
- Business logic in the client (step, policy, closure, action gating).
- Visual or copy changes from the prototype.

Acceptance criteria:
- Approve is disabled with the stale banner when the packet is out of date.
- Return requires the reason before the action enables.
- Policy shows the human label; failed rules link to evidence.
- Parity passes for decide, waiting on reviewer, returned and stale states.

Dependencies:
- VER-012-F4
- VER-009

Test-first behavior:

Demo proof:
Send to Dana, approve, then replay with a material change.
