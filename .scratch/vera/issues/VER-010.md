Title: VER-010 — Credit, dispute and card controls

User value:
After approval, Sam issues the simulated credit exactly once, prepares the dispute, and the card admin approves and applies controls — in parallel and without duplicates.

Scope:
- provisional_credits, dispute_packets, control_plans, card_controls; simulated credit, dispute and card-control adapters with lookup by key and fault injection.
- Commands: issue_provisional_credit, lookup_execution_result, prepare_dispute_packet, open_dispute_packet, review_dispute_packet, submit_dispute, propose_card_controls, skip_card_controls, approve_card_controls, decline_card_controls, apply_card_controls.
- Prevention Planner and Dispute Packet Preparer on the specialist runner; parallel tracks per decision D3.
- adapter_executor and uncertain_result_reconciler jobs; `GET /api/cases/{id}/executions`.

Non-goals:
- Real money, networks or card systems.
- Final communication and closure.

Acceptance criteria:
- issue_provisional_credit requires an eligible policy result, a current packet and a current bound approval; a stale approval is rejected.
- The idempotency key is committed before the adapter is called; two simultaneous requests produce one credit (fixture 16).
- A failed-before-effect result leaves nothing issued and allows retry; an uncertain result puts the case in credit_check where only lookup is offered, and lookup resolves to one credit (fixture 17).
- Charge impact shows the credit and recalculated exposure, labeled simulated.
- Dispute status moves prepared, ready_for_review, ready_to_submit, submitted, with task, chat and activity in agreement; a failed submission retries to one submission (fixtures 33, 34).
- The dispute can progress while the control plan is unsent, and sending the plan does not reset the dispute (fixture 44).
- approve_card_controls and apply_card_controls require card_admin; analyst and reviewer-only sessions get 403; apply before approve is rejected; apply twice replays (fixture 35).
- Events for approval of the decision carry role reviewer and control events carry role card_admin for the same person (fixture 45).
- A decision on CHG-1042 creates no credit, approval or control on CHG-1051.

Dependencies:
- VER-009

Test-first behavior:
A concurrency test firing two issue_provisional_credit commands and asserting one credit row and one adapter effect; then the uncertain-result path.

Demo proof:
Issue the credit, show the ledger, prepare the dispute while Dana approves and applies controls as card admin.
