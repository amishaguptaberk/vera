Title: VER-012-F5 — Communications and cardholder request

User value:
Requests, replies and attachments live inside the case, and Priya gets a simple page to answer.

Scope:
- Communications list and message detail, DraftEditor with drafts store, send confirmation with channel, subject, attachments and case id; cardholder request route with answer options, note, attachments and send.
- Follow docs/frontend-architecture.md for layer rules, state ownership and parity method; the prototype is the visual reference and is not redesigned.

Non-goals:
- Identity and call detail.
- Business logic in the client (step, policy, closure, action gating).
- Visual or copy changes from the prototype.

Acceptance criteria:
- Send is disabled until the backend offers it; draft text survives refetch and refresh.
- The cardholder page shows no internal ids or specialist names.
- Files over 10 MB or of a disallowed type show the inline error and keep the draft.
- The reply appears under the original request marked New.
- Parity passes for draft, waiting, overdue and response states, and the cardholder page at 390 px.

Dependencies:
- VER-012-F3
- VER-005

Test-first behavior:
A test for the cardholder form's disabled and error states.

Demo proof:
Send the request, answer as Priya, see the reply in the case.
