Title: VER-009 — Decision packet and reviewer action

User value:
Dana can decide from one packet that separates evidence, conflicts, gaps and policy, and her approval only counts for the exact evidence she saw.

Scope:
- Decision Packet Preparer on the specialist runner; decision_packets versioned and immutable; decisions; approvals with binding and status.
- Commands: create_decision_packet, request_reviewer_approval, record_decision, approve_decision, return_decision (reason, requested next step and owner — gap G2), remind_reviewer, reassign_reviewer.
- `GET /api/cases/{id}/decision-packet?version=`; reviewer-scoped CaseView; read-only `POST /api/cases/{id}/ask` with the required canned answers and deduplication.

Non-goals:
- Credit execution.
- Card controls.

Acceptance criteria:
- The packet records snapshot hash, workflow version and policy version; creating it approves nothing (US-F01).
- Approval binds case, transaction, snapshot hash, policy version and workflow version; double approval yields one approval (US-F03).
- Approving a packet whose hash differs from the current snapshot returns 422 and the approval is shown as stale (fixtures 14, 15).
- Return without a reason returns 422; a returned packet is unchanged and a new version can be prepared (US-F04).
- The analyst cannot approve; the reviewer cannot issue credit.
- Asking Vera to approve returns the canned refusal, leaves the approval pending and writes no audit event (fixture 31).
- Repeating the same question replaces the earlier pair and writes no audit event (fixture 32).
- Answers cite permitted source records.

Dependencies:
- VER-007
- VER-008

Test-first behavior:
A test that requests approval, applies a material change, and asserts approve_decision is rejected and the step is `stale`.

Demo proof:
Send the packet to Dana, ask Vera why credit is blocked, approve explicitly, and show the binding.
