Title: VER-015 — Dashboard exposure: gross and net

User value:
The analyst sees how much is under review in total (gross) and how much remains after money that has actually been recovered (net), without pending or estimated amounts being mixed into either number.

Scope:
- Replace the single `open_exposure` summary metric with `gross_exposure` (primary) and `net_exposure`, plus `confirmed_recoveries` and `temporary_credit`, per plan §19 D-H.
- Each summary amount carries a `basis` of verified, pending or estimated and a one-line definition string the UI can show.
- Distinguish issued (pending) credit from finalized (confirmed) credit in storage so net can only deduct confirmed recoveries.
- Due-today and overdue amounts are gross.

Non-goals:
- The dashboard UI (VER-012-F2).
- Credit issuance and finalization commands (VER-010, VER-011).

Acceptance criteria:
- [x] Gross exposure equals the sum of posted amounts of all non-closed cases, including cases with a temporary credit.
- [x] Net exposure equals gross exposure minus confirmed recoveries only; an issued but unfinalized temporary credit does not reduce it.
- [x] Temporary credit is reported separately with basis pending; confirmed recoveries with basis verified.
- [x] No metric sums amounts of different bases; an estimated amount never appears in gross or net exposure.
- [x] With the seed data, gross exposure is 1122000 cents across six cases and CHG-1036's 396000 temporary credit is reported as pending and not deducted.
- [x] Finalizing a credit in the database moves its amount from temporary credit to confirmed recoveries and reduces net exposure by exactly that amount.
- [x] Every summary metric includes its definition and basis in the response.

Dependencies:
- VER-002

Test-first behavior:
A test asserting the seeded summary: gross 1122000 (6 cases), net 1122000, temporary credit 396000 pending, confirmed recoveries 0; then marking CHG-1036's credit finalized and asserting net drops by 396000 while gross is unchanged.

Demo proof:
Call the summary endpoint before and after finalizing a credit and show the four figures with their bases.
