Title: VER-012-F3 — Case workspace shell and task card

User value:
Sam always sees one task and exactly the actions the backend allows, with state, owner and due at a glance.

Scope:
- CaseHeader, StatusRail, ProgressSteps, CaseTabs, TaskCard blocks, ActionBar rendered from available_actions, ChargeImpact; runCommand with server-supplied idempotency key and expected version; SSE subscription with polling fallback.
- Follow docs/frontend-architecture.md for layer rules, state ownership and parity method; the prototype is the visual reference and is not redesigned.

Non-goals:
- Tab contents.
- Business logic in the client (step, policy, closure, action gating).
- Visual or copy changes from the prototype.

Acceptance criteria:
- Buttons are exactly available_actions in order; index 0 is the only primary; no client mapping from step to buttons.
- A 409 stale_case_version refetches and does not auto-retry.
- Blocked reasons render inline under the actions.
- Tab and selection live in the URL and survive refresh (US-J06).
- The workspace never blanks during a transition; parity passes for intake, hold and resume states.

Dependencies:
- VER-012-F1
- VER-002

Test-first behavior:
A test that renders ActionBar from an ActionView list and asserts labels, order and disabled reasons.

Demo proof:
Hold and resume the case from the UI.
