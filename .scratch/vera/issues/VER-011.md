Title: VER-011 — Final communication and closure

User value:
Priya is told only what actually happened, and the case cannot be closed while required work is open.

Scope:
- Final communication drafted from recorded state; content policy that allows only recorded actions.
- Closure checklist (Handoff §4.3) as a server computation; commands draft_final_message, send_communication (final), close_case, record_dispute_outcome.
- Monitoring and closed states; closed-case rejection at gateway stage 5; dispute_outcome_ingest demo job.

Non-goals:
- Reopening a closed case.

Acceptance criteria:
- A final message that mentions a credit, dispute or control that is not recorded is rejected with 422.
- close_case with open items returns 422 listing each item with owner and next action (US-G08).
- Closing with a submitted dispute enters monitoring; recording the outcome closes the case and finalizes or reverses the credit.
- Closing records outcome, actor, timestamp and evidence snapshot.
- Any other command on a closed or monitoring case is rejected with case_closed and writes no audit event (fixture 20).
- The final message is sent once.

Dependencies:
- VER-010

Test-first behavior:
A test that attempts close_case before the final message and asserts the 422 item list; then completes the path and asserts closed-case rejection.

Demo proof:
Send the final update, close into monitoring, record the dispute outcome and show the durable decision record.
