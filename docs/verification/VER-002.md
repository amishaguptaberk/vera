## Known limitations

- Round-3 repair unreviewed (above). Edge cases the stopped reviewer was asked to attack remain open: an id with a trailing newline, the largest nine-digit id.

- Policy and permission gates (stages 7–8) are no-op hooks until VER-008.
- `recovery_actions` is always empty until failures exist.
- Replay precedes the version, closed and step gates, so a completed key replays even on a closed case.
- Zero-width-space-only reasons are accepted. Statement length is unbounded.
- `report:{id}` keys are global, not per case.
