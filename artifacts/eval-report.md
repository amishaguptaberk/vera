## Known limitations

- All cases run on the single synthetic seed (CHG-1042 and its related records). Variation comes from commands, demo seams (material change, adapter faults, call outcomes, deadline watcher) and scripted specialist turns, not from a corpus of distinct customers.
- Specialists run in REPLAY from reference recordings. A case that needs the model to say something else (a voicemail, a contradicted call) overrides that turn with an 'eval-scripted' recording; it still passes through the same loop and validators. Live model quality is not measured by this suite.
- Where a stale approval moves the case to decision_pending/stale, a credit attempt is refused by the step gate first; the approval-binding gate behind it is exercised directly in tests/integration/test_decisions.py.
- A 'dispute' decision goes straight to the dispute track without reviewer approval; the dispute is gated by the analyst-reviewed packet. This is the product's current rule and is recorded, not changed, by this suite.
- Webhook cases use the ElevenLabs provider over an in-process mock transport with real signatures; no telephony provider is contacted.
- The worker is driven in-process with a synthetic clock (2031-01-01 onward); real concurrency between worker processes is covered by tests/integration/test_worker_graph.py.
- The optional LLM judge scores only bounded qualitative text and never runs in CI; it needs NVIDIA_API_KEY and --judge.
